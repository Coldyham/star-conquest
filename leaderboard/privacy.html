Privacy

Star Conquest collects as little as it can. You can play, post scores and watch replays without an account. This page says what is kept, where it is kept, and how to have it removed.

Last updated 5 October 2026.

Who runs this site

Star Conquest, the game and this leaderboard, is run by Coldyham, who is responsible for the data described here. To ask about it, or to exercise any of the rights below, contact Coldyham on GitHub.

No tracking

There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts. The pages, scripts and fonts are all served from this site.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional, and it does one thing: it lets you claim a name so nobody else can post under it.

  • When you sign in, Google passes your email address, name, profile picture link and Google account ID to Supabase, the service that runs sign-in for this site. Google always sends the name and picture as part of a standard sign-in, but this site never uses or shows them.
  • Supabase also records each sign-in: when it happened, your IP address and your browser's user agent.
  • The leaderboard links your claimed name to your account's ID number. That link is not public. The board shows only that a name is claimed, never who claimed it.
  • Your email is used to show you who you are signed in as. If you ask for a name you have already been posting under, the site's owner also uses it to find your account so the name can be assigned to you. It is never shown to anyone else, and nobody is ever emailed.

Why: a claimed name needs an account to belong to. How long: until you ask for your account to be deleted.

Scores, names and replays

Everything you post to the board is public. That means the name you enter, the map, your turns, ships lost, how many turns you played yourself, the name of whoever challenged you, and when you posted. Anyone can watch the replay of a posted score. A replay holds the moves of the game and no name or account. If you tick Share replays in the game, it also uploads checkpoints of your games, and those stay private unless a posted score points at them.

For a shared (play-by-post) match, the server keeps the match setup, every seat's orders, the match title and the name each player gives their seat. Your seat's link holds a secret token, and the server stores only a hash of it, never the token itself. A public match is listed in the lobby along with its seat names.

Any name you type is public, so choose one you are happy for anyone to see.

Why: posting a score or joining a match is what you came to do, and a fair public leaderboard needs its scores to be checkable. How long: until removed. Ask, and the scores under your name will be deleted.

Your IP address

Netlify hosts this site. Like any web host, it sees your IP address on every request and keeps standard request logs under its own policy. The site's upload and shared-match endpoints also check your IP address, in memory only, to limit how fast one visitor can send. It is never written to the database.

Why: to keep the site running and stop it being flooded.

On your device

The game and the board keep some things in your browser's local storage: your settings, your best scores, the game in progress and saved replays, your shared-match seat links, your preferences (such as animated turns and Share replays) and, if you sign in, your sign-in session and email. None of this is a tracking cookie. Each item leaves your device only when you use the feature it belongs to. Signing out removes the session, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes the rest.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase runs the database and sign-in. Its servers for this site are in eu-west-2 (London).
  • Netlify hosts the site and its server functions.
  • Google handles the sign-in step itself. What Google does with your account is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Supabase and Netlify handle this data only on the site's behalf, under their data processing terms. Those terms include the standard safeguards for data held outside the UK.

Children

Signing in is for people aged 13 and over. The rest of the site needs no account and collects nothing about who you are.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the data held about you, have it corrected or deleted, limit or object to how it is used, or take a copy of it. Contact Coldyham on GitHub. Deleting your account also frees your claimed name. To give up a name and keep your account, use Your name.

If you are unhappy with how a request was handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If anything here changes, this page and its date change with it.
