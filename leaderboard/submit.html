Enter a score, or share a setup

Win a game and press Enter on leaderboard — this page opens with your result already filled in, and you just add a name. Or press Challenge a friend and paste the link it copies here.

A plain settings-share link works too, with nothing to post yet: it registers the setup and gives you a page to link people to. It stays off the board itself until someone posts a score on it.

Only takes effect the first time this exact map reaches the board. Scores and rankings still post and rank normally the whole time — this hides replays only, so nobody (you included) can watch one back until the embargo lifts.

Posting a score also publishes that game's replay, so anyone can watch it back on the map's page.

Tags describe the setup you just played (e.g. "grindy", "fast games") and only count while attached to a posted score.
