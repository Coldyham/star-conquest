Daily puzzle

Board Puzzle

A small duel a day, the same for everyone, new at midnight UTC, with no fog. Winning is the easy part: the puzzle is winning in the fewest turns, then with the fewest ships lost. Each weekday has its own opponent, the same every week. Scores and replays stay hidden until the day ends: the best score posted on the day wins it. A streak is a run of days won; a missed day won later mends it, and the true streak, days won on the day itself, shows when you hover or tap.
