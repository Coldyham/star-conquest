Make a gauntlet

Public Make new

String maps together into a run for friends, a club or a class. Each stage opens once the one before it is won, or won within its target if it has one.

An admin decides what is listed. A generated gauntlet is made in your browser by the game, and it is sent to the board, with your name, only when this is ticked; otherwise it stays in the browser that made it.

Pick the rules and the game picks the maps, ordered easiest to hardest by test bots playing each one. It runs in your browser, so more stages take longer. Left without a name, a gauntlet is named after its seed.
